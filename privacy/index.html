Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the games published by PalathGame (“we”, “us”) on Google Play collect, how it is used and what choices you have. The general part applies to all our games. Each game has its own section below that lists the services it uses.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number or location, and we do not sell personal information. Our games do not require an account.

  • Data stored on your device. Game progress, settings, unlocked items and in-game currency are saved locally on your device. We cannot access this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Data processed by third-party services. Some games use the services listed in their section below (for example advertising or online leaderboards). These services may collect device identifiers and technical information as described in their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

Games with ads use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and process the advertising ID of your device, your IP address, device and app information, and information about your interaction with ads, in order to show, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

Unity Gaming Services (online leaderboards)

Games with online leaderboards use Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards). When you start the game, an anonymous player account is created for your installation. If you take part in a leaderboard, your score and the player name you choose in the game are stored by Unity and are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a player name. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Uninstalling a game deletes all data stored on your device.
  • To remove your leaderboard entry and player name, e-mail us at the address below, with the player name you used and the game's name.
  • You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
