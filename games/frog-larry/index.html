Frog Larry: Homecoming

A coding puzzle game for kids

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Frog Larry got lost in the forest. Help him find the way home to his pond! Drag command blocks (Move, Jump, Turn left, Turn right and Eat) into a row, press Play and watch Larry follow your program step by step. Jump over stones, find a way around the plants and catch every fly on the way.

Without noticing, children learn the basics of programming: planning a sequence of steps, finding and fixing mistakes, and using loops to repeat commands, even loops inside loops.

Trailer

Features

  • 50 hand-made levels in five chapters, from the first step to tricky spirals.
  • Guided tutorial that shows every new block, including the nested repeater.
  • Up to three stars per level: reach the pond, stay within the move limit and catch every fly.
  • Frog shop: flies you catch buy new colours for Larry.
  • Four languages: English, Czech, German and French.
  • No ads, no account, works offline.

For parents

Frog Larry has no advertising and no links out of the game, and it collects no personal data. Progress is saved only on the device. Once a level is finished, the game may show Google Play's own rating window. See the privacy section for Frog Larry.
