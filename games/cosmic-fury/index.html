Cosmic Fury

A horizontal space shooter

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Take the controls of a fighter ship and hold off an endless enemy fleet. Steer with the joystick, fire your guns and launch rockets, and dodge everything the enemy throws at you.

The longer you survive, the harder it gets: new enemy types join the attack, whole formations sweep across the screen and every so often a boss arrives. Kill enemies quickly one after another to build a combo, collect coins and spend them on better ships and upgrades.

Features

  • Endless survival with nine enemy types and attack formations.
  • Eight different bosses, each with its own attack.
  • Combo multiplier up to x5 for fast kills in a row.
  • Power-ups: coin magnet, double score and a helper drone, plus pickups for faster and stronger shots and rockets, healing and a shield.
  • Eight ships to unlock and seven upgradeable stats for each of them.
  • Daily rewards: a seven-day login streak, daily and weekly missions and a daily challenge with its own rules.
  • Online leaderboards: an all-time ranking and a weekly tournament where the top five win coins.
  • Three difficulty levels and achievements.

Ads, purchases and privacy

The game is free and shows ads. Some extras, like continuing a game, are unlocked by watching an optional ad. Coin packs and an option to disable the forced ads can be bought in the shop through Google Play. Your player name and score are sent to the online leaderboards, everything else stays on your device.
