U.F.O. Invasion

A side-scrolling space shooter

Get it on Google Play Privacy

Aliens are invading! Hold your finger on the screen and your ship flies towards it while it shoots automatically. Dodge enemy shots, kamikaze ships and lasers, and shoot down wave after wave of alien ships as the sky turns from day to night and the snow starts to fall.

The longer you survive, the tougher it gets: new enemy types join the fight and sooner or later a boss arrives. Kill enemies quickly one after another to build a combo and multiply your score.

Features

  • Endless survival with nine enemy types that join as your score grows.
  • Eight different bosses, each with its own attack.
  • Combo multiplier: fast kills in a row give x2 and x3 score and more coins.
  • Power-ups: green ones help you, red ones hurt you. Shield and coin magnet too.
  • Eight ships to unlock with coins, each with its own small bonus.
  • Three difficulty levels, achievements and a daily reward.
  • Online leaderboard: compare your best score with other players.

Ads and privacy

The game is free and shows ads. Some extras, like continuing a game, are unlocked by watching an optional ad. Your nickname and score are sent to the online leaderboard, everything else stays on your device. See the privacy section for U.F.O. Invasion.
